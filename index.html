Banana Fibre Revolution

Maleema transforms banana agricultural waste into premium natural textile fibre, creating sustainable materials for fashion, textiles, and eco-friendly products.

About Maleema

Maleema is a sustainable textile initiative focused on converting banana crop waste into valuable natural fibre. By transforming agricultural residue into textile materials, we reduce environmental impact while creating opportunities for farmers and traditional weaving communities.

Material Innovation

Banana Fibre

Extracted from banana stems after harvest, turning agricultural waste into a valuable biodegradable textile resource.

Low Water Use

Compared to cotton, banana fibre production requires minimal additional water, making it a more sustainable alternative.

Biodegradable

Banana fibre products naturally decompose without contributing to microplastic pollution.

Rural Impact

Supports farmers and weaving communities by creating new income from agricultural waste.
