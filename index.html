Why Banana Fibre

Nature's Most
Underestimated Fibre

One plant. One harvest. An entirely new fibre supply chain built from waste the world has been ignoring for generations.

💧

Low Water Footprint

Banana is cultivated for its fruit, and the fibre comes entirely from the waste stem requiring minimal water and no additional irrigation or environmental footprint.

💪

Stronger Than Jute

Banana fibre has 2× the tensile strength of jute with a natural silk like lustre. Moisture wicking, antimicrobial, and beautifully soft at fine processing grades.

🌍

Massive Scale Potential

India grows millions of tonnes of bananas annually. The pseudostem waste is enormous and almost entirely untapped. Maleema is turning that into a global supply chain.

♻️

Fully Circular

From agricultural waste to finished product to end of life, every Maleema material is 100% biodegradable composting fully in under 2 years, zero residue.

👩‍🌾

Farmer-First Value

We pay smallholder farmers for stem waste, turning a disposal problem into a 30% seasonal income boost. Women lead our processing workforce.

🧬

Naturally Antimicrobial

Inherent antimicrobial properties make banana fibre ideal for medical textiles, hygiene applications, and sensitive skin without any chemical treatment.

How It's Made

Field Waste to
Finished Fibre

Five steps. One plant. No waste. Every banana stem becomes a premium material engineered to use less and give more at every stage.

STEP 01
🌿

Collect

Pseudostem from partner farms. Zero new crops, zero new land.

STEP 02
⚙️

Extract

Mechanical decortication releases long cellulosic fibres cleanly.

STEP 03
☀️

Grade

Sun dried and sorted into 12+ grades by fineness and strength.

STEP 04
🧶

Spin

Cottonized fibre spun into yarn or wet laid into cellulose sheets.

STEP 05
📦

Fabric

Fibres woven into durable, breathable fabrics ready for sustainable products.

Vision & Mission
"The banana plant produces its fruit and the stem is often discarded. Yet within that stem lies one of nature’s finest fibres. MaLeeMa bridges the gap between what farmers leave behind and what the textile industry urgently needs.
— Maleema Brand Statement

A World Where
Waste Weaves

We envision a global textile industry where agricultural by products replace virgin crops as the primary source of natural fibre. A world where every banana harvested also yields premium material and no stem is ever burned again.

Maleema's mission is to transform banana crop waste into the world's most accessible natural fibre alternative creating income for smallholder farmers and a genuine scalable replacement across yarn, cellulose sheets, medical textiles, home textiles, fashion, and knit.

We are not making a compromise product. We are making the better product.
